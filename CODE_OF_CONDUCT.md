# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

TaskFlow contributors, maintainers, and assessment participants are expected to create a respectful, professional, and inclusive environment. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and harassment are not.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly, respectfully, and with empathy.
- Give actionable feedback focused on code, evidence, and outcomes.
- Respect candidate privacy, assessment integrity, and intellectual effort.
- Credit others' work and disclose relevant sources.
- Accept correction, take responsibility, and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing private information, credentials, reports, or candidate submissions without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, assessment manipulation, or deliberate disruption of repository services.
- Repeatedly derailing technical collaboration after a maintainer asks for the behavior to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, automation, and other TieOpp spaces connected to this project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or escalate platform abuse when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the authenticated TieOpp support channel associated with your account. For immediate GitHub platform abuse, use GitHub's reporting tools. Do not place sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, consider the safety of affected people, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
